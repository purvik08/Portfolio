Our Story

Built by Students,

Axyntra Robotics was born from a simple frustration — quality robotics hardware was either too expensive or too closed to learn from. We decided to change that.

Based in Surat, Gujarat, we build every product with education at its core. Not just a device you buy — but a device you truly understand, modify, and build upon.
